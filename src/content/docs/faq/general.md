## Is Epsilos a SaaS platform?

Yes. With the standard e-commerce and ready website packages, Epsilos is a fully
managed service: hosting, servers, updates and backups are on Epsilos; you manage
your store and content from a browser-based panel.

## Can I install it on my own server?

The standard packages do not include installation on your own server; Epsilos handles
hosting. Different models exist for enterprise and reseller scenarios: contact the
sales team.

## Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You can connect your existing domain or register a new one through Epsilos.

## Do I own my data?

Yes. Your content and data are yours and can be exported under the terms of your
contract.

## Does my site depend on Epsilos being online?

No. Your licence is validated locally on your own site: the licence key it holds
is checked with a cryptographic signature, without contacting Epsilos on each
request. If Epsilos's infrastructure were unreachable, your site keeps running.
With the managed packages hosting is on Epsilos anyway; this matters most when you
run Epsilos on your own or a partner's infrastructure.

## Can my licence be used on another site?

No. Your packages and add-ons are licensed to your domain. A licence key issued for
one installation will not activate on a different domain, so your setup cannot be
copied or resold.

## Does the licence expire?

A licence has a term and continues by renewal, like a subscription. With the
managed packages Epsilos handles renewal; if you self-host, you enter the renewed
licence key in the panel.

## Which payment methods are supported?

Standard card schemes (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) plus regional payment
gateways with installment support, and multi-currency pricing with live exchange
rates. Available gateways depend on the markets you sell in.

## Do I need technical knowledge?

No. All management is done from a visual panel. Pages are built with a drag-and-drop
designer, no coding required.

## Can I change the theme later?

Yes. The active theme is switched instantly from the panel; your content and data are
not affected.

## Can I try it first?

Yes. You can open a free demo account and get a personalised presentation from the
Epsilos team: [epsilos.com](https://www.epsilos.com).

## How is support provided?

Email support (support@epsilos.com) with a response time within 12 hours, plus live
chat during business hours. Every account also gets a personalised onboarding
presentation.
