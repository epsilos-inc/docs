Epsilos e-commerce packages include what is needed to sell across borders from a
single store — there is no separate "export" edition.

## What is included

- **Multi-language storefront and checkout** — content, product data and the
  checkout flow per language, with automatic redirect by browser language and
  right-to-left (RTL) support.
- **Multi-currency pricing** — prices shown and charged in the visitor's currency
  with live exchange rates, or fixed per-market prices you set.
- **Regional payment gateways** — connect the payment methods that matter in each
  market you sell to.
- **Country and region settings** — control which countries you sell to and vary
  behaviour by region.
- **Regional shipping** — carrier options and rates per destination.
- **Regional tax** — tax rules and rates by country or region.

## B2C and B2B

All of the above works the same for a **B2B storefront** — dealer and wholesale
pricing, per-market currencies and payment methods, and region-specific catalogs.
