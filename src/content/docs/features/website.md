Ready Website packages are for businesses that need a corporate or showcase site
without an e-commerce store.

## Visual page builder

A drag-and-drop layout designer that works directly on the live site. Pages are
built with a 4-level nested grid: **Grid → Row → Column → Module**.

35+ ready-made modules: product / content sliders, hero banners, image galleries,
dynamic menus, heading and text blocks, video popups, pricing tables, countdown
timers, contact forms, social media widgets, Google Maps, tab panels and more. Each
module has its own settings panel opened on the live canvas.

## Content

- Blog module (posts, categories, tags, comments)
- Multi-language content
- Contact forms
- Reusable header / footer sections
- Menu / navigation builder

## Themes

- 37+ corporate and showcase themes
- Categories: corporate, architecture & construction, health, law & consulting,
  restaurant, technology & SaaS, education and more
- All themes mobile-responsive; instant switching from the panel

## SEO and integration

- Per-page meta title, description, keywords, Open Graph
- Google Analytics / Ads
- Custom code injection slots (GTM, chat widgets)
- SMTP email
