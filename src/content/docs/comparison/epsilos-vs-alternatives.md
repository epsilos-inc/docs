There are several good ways to run an online store or corporate website. This page
explains where Epsilos fits among the common alternatives and gives you a checklist
to decide. We recommend trying any shortlist with a demo — the right platform
depends on your products, market and team.

## The landscape

| Platform | In short |
| --- | --- |
| **Epsilos** | Turkish managed platform for **both** e-commerce and corporate/showcase sites, with a live drag-and-drop page builder, 70+ themes, local payment support and a reseller/designer ecosystem. |
| **İkas** | Turkish managed e-commerce platform with a modern admin and a growing feature set, focused on online retail. |
| **Ticimax / İdeasoft** | Established Turkish e-commerce platforms with deep retail feature sets and long track records in the local market. |
| **Shopify** | Global e-commerce platform with the largest third-party app ecosystem; strongest for businesses selling internationally. |
| **Wix** | Global website builder with e-commerce add-ons; strong for content and marketing sites, lighter for large catalogs. |

All of these are managed/hosted — the provider runs the infrastructure and you work
through a browser panel.

## Where Epsilos is a strong fit

- **One platform for a store *and* a corporate site** — same panel, same themes,
  no second system.
- **Visual, live page design** — build pages by drag-and-drop directly on the site,
  with 35+ modules, not just a fixed template.
- **Turkish market fit** — PayTR and Iyzico with installments, Turkish Central Bank
  exchange rates, Turkish interface and support.
- **Vertical modules** — hotel booking, real-estate listings, QR menu, e-invoicing
  and more through the [add-on marketplace](https://market.epsilos.com).
- **Reseller & designer programs** — build a business on top of Epsilos.

## Where another platform may fit better

- **Selling heavily internationally, in many currencies and languages, with niche
  integrations** — Shopify's app ecosystem is hard to match.
- **A pure content/marketing site with minimal commerce** — a lighter website
  builder may be enough.
- **You already run a large, deeply customised store on another Turkish platform**
  and migration cost outweighs the benefit.

## How to choose — a checklist

1. **Scope:** do you need only a store, only a site, or both?
2. **Where you sell:** mostly Turkey, or mostly abroad?
3. **Design control:** template-based, or hands-on visual layout?
4. **Catalog size and complexity:** simple, or many variants / bulk import?
5. **Special needs:** a vertical module (hotel, real estate, restaurant)?
6. **Team:** who maintains it, and how technical are they?
7. **Total cost:** subscription + payment fees + any add-ons.

Then shortlist two or three and run a demo of each with your own products.
